Icon System · v1

A unified outline icon set replacing every emoji across the product — drawn on shared geometry, monochrome with a single indigo accent. Brand integrations keep their authentic marks. Every icon below is shown in three directions so you can mix and match.

36 icons 3 style directions 16 / 20 / 24 grid 5 brand marks Monochrome + indigo accent

The problem

Emoji render differently on every OS, can't be recolored, break visual rhythm, and look unprofessional in a QA tooling product. They were used for nav, features, status, and actions alike.

The approach

One geometric system: 24px grid, consistent stroke, rounded joints. Functional icons are monochrome so they inherit text color; a single indigo accent marks the meaningful part.

Brand handling

Jira, GitHub, Cypress, Slack and Docker keep their real logos and colors — users trust recognizable marks for integrations. Everything else is custom and on-brand.
